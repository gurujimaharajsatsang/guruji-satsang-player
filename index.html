About the Satsang Player

What this is

The Guruji Ka Satsang Player is a simple web page, offered humbly as seva, to help prepare the shabad playlist for satsang. It is the maryada of our satsang that only shabads are played — and only those that Guruji had selected and played. This player makes preparing such a satsang effortless.

Why it was made

Often, in the last hour before satsang, there is a hurry to arrange the playlist, and whatever is found online is played — sometimes with advertisements interrupting those sacred moments. This player was made so the sangat no longer has to scramble, and so the satsang stays pure: only Guruji's chosen shabads, playing from beginning to end without a single interruption.

Features

  • Duration-aware Auto-Fill — choose how long the satsang is (1 hour, 2 hours, or your own custom time) and the player fills a suitable playlist of shabads to fit.
  • Ek Onkar is set automatically — every satsang playlist opens with Ek Onkar, as it should.
  • Mantra Jaap is set automatically — the playlist closes with the Mantra Jaap, so the satsang ends the proper way without you arranging it.
  • Aarti kept separate — the Aarti is not mixed into the playlist. When the satsang reaches that point, you simply play the Aarti when ready — no need to pause or interrupt the player.
  • Shabad Library — the full collection of Guruji's selected shabads, searchable, ready to add to the satsang playlist or reorder as needed.
  • Even, balanced volume — the player evens out the loudness across shabads, so no track plays suddenly louder or softer than another.
  • No ads, no interruptions — the satsang plays smoothly from start to finish.
  • Photo slideshow of Guruji — a gentle slideshow of Guruji's swaroop plays while the shabads are heard.
  • Drag to reorder — arrange the playlist just as you wish by dragging shabads into order.
  • Shuffle & Pick One — play the library shabads in a random order, or let Pick One choose a single shabad for you.
  • Works as a standalone shabad player — play shabads from the library anytime, with or without building a satsang playlist.
  • Two colour themes — a rose theme and an orange theme; tap the theme button to switch.

How to use it

Preparing a Satsang Playlist

  1. Set the duration — choose 1 Hour, 2 Hours, or Custom for the length of your satsang.
  2. Tap Auto-Fill — the player builds a complete playlist of shabads to fit that time, with Ek Onkar at the start and Mantra Jaap at the end.
  3. Adjust if you wish — search the Shabad Library to add particular shabads, and drag shabads to reorder them.
  4. Press play — the satsang plays through smoothly on its own.
  5. For the Aarti — when the satsang reaches the Aarti, simply play it from the Aarti section when you are ready.

Using the Shabad Library

The Shabad Library also works on its own as a standalone shabad player — you do not need to build a satsang playlist to simply listen to shabads.

  • Search the library for any shabad and play it directly.
  • Add to the satsang — send any shabad from the library into the Satsang Playlist.
  • Shuffle — turn Shuffle on to play the shabads of the library in a random order.
  • Pick One — plays a single shabad chosen at random, then stops. A gentle way to hear just one shabad.

Playing a shabad from the library does not begin the satsang — the satsang starts only when you press play on the Satsang Playlist itself.
